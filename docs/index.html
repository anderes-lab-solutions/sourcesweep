    State of the field

    With zolbetuximab already in routine use S85S103, the field's momentum has moved to antibody-drug conjugates (ADCs). AstraZeneca's sonesitatug vedotin improved overall survival in 2L+ CLDN18.2-positive gastric cancer in CLARITY-Gastric01, presented at ESMO 2026, and the US FDA granted it Priority Review S230S231S250S257. Headlines quote different risk reductions (42% in AstraZeneca's release vs 53% in one news report), so check the primary data S231S248. Arcotatug tavatecan (TAK-921), the ADC behind Takeda's $1.2B Innovent deal, also met its Phase 3 endpoints, and Takeda is preparing filings S227S238S244S246S255. AstraZeneca's AZD0901 S10S126 and Innovent's IBI343 S45S48 have their own late-stage and combination trials.

    Combinations and new formats are building behind the ADCs. AstraZeneca invested $2 billion in Summit Therapeutics and will test sonesitatug vedotin with ivonescimab in GI cancers S208S212S243. Bispecifics (givastomig, ASP2138) S3S18S210S241, CAR-T (satri-cel/CT041, now on a Chinese commercial insurance list) S75S117S236, and Phase 3 programs from Chinese sponsors (ATG-022, SHR-A1904, JS107) S6S20S51 fill the pipeline.

    Testing is a recurring theme. CLDN18.2 status can change after systemic therapy in both gastric and pancreatic cancer S139S140. Scoring agreement for zolbetuximab eligibility is substantial but weakest at low staining intensities S159.

    Biology & biomarker signals

    • CLDN18.2 status is dynamic: in 155 paired gastric/EGJ samples, 21.78% of negatives converted to positive after therapy and 27.78% of positives converted to negative S139. In pancreatic cancer, positivity fell from 41.9% to 24.3% after chemotherapy S140. Both support re-testing before targeted therapy.
    • Six GI pathologists showed substantial agreement on zolbetuximab eligibility, with the most variability at weaker staining S159. Japanese sites test CLDN18 in-house far less often than HER2 S189.
    • Circulating extracellular-vesicle proteins tracked, and could interfere with, CLDN18.2 CAR-T and antibody efficacy in CT041 and GLOW trial samples S141.
    • AI and imaging approaches to predict CLDN18.2 status are emerging (slide-based deep learning, radiomics, PET), though still early S12S14S164S270.
